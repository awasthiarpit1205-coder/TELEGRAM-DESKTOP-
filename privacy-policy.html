2. Core Nature of Website & Services

Session King operates purely as an educational, statistical analysis, and historical performance tracking platform designed for sports journalists, cricket enthusiasts, and data analysts.

Our work entails:

  • Pitch & Ground Intel: Historical venue analysis, average par scores, surface degradation tendencies, and weather influence.
  • Session Dynamics: Statistical breakdown of phase-wise scoring rhythms (e.g., Powerplay vs. Middle Overs vs. Death Overs) across recognized tournaments.
  • Head-to-Head Records: Past match data, bowler-batter matchup matrices, and player career trajectories.
  • Informational Community: A free public discussion channel on Telegram where match previews and statistical summaries are shared for study and discussion.
